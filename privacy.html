SATCOM Video Studio — Privacy Policy

Effective September 30, 2026. This policy describes how SATCOM Video Studio uses Google and YouTube data. It applies to the internal tool at satcom.conews.press/video/studio.

What this app is

SATCOM Video Studio is an internal, password-protected newsroom tool. It is used only by our own staff to review and publish our own news videos. It is not a public product and is not offered to other organizations or end users.

Google and YouTube data we access

Staff connect our own YouTube channel with Google OAuth. We request only these scopes:

  • youtube.upload — upload videos to our channel
  • youtube.force-ssl — update title, description, and privacy status, and delete videos on our channel

We use that access only to upload, update (title, description, privacy status), and delete videos on our own YouTube channel. We do not access other users' channels or data.

What we store

We store an encrypted (AES-256-GCM) OAuth refresh token for our own channel, the IDs of videos we uploaded, and basic publish audit logs. We do not collect or store personal data from viewers or other users.

How we do not use this data

We do not sell, rent, or share Google user data. We do not use it for advertising. Google user data is not used to train AI models.

Google API and YouTube terms

SATCOM Video Studio's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

The Studio uses YouTube API Services. Use of those services is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

You can revoke SATCOM Video Studio's access at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. When staff disconnect YouTube in the Studio, we delete the stored tokens.

Retention

OAuth tokens are kept until the channel is disconnected. Publish audit logs are kept for up to 1 year.

Contact

Questions about this policy: support@villagerpublishing.com.
