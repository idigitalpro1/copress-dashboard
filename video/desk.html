Flow

  1. Intake: Paul Hill clips, Omni drafts, operator uploads (automatic registration)
  2. Process: transcode, transcript/captions, AI titles, descriptions and thumbnail candidates, AI label, rights pre-check (automatic drafts)
  3. Review: an editor checks captions, copy, rights/consent and the AI label (human)
  4. Publish: YouTube (unlisted first), satcom.conews.press/video, social drafts (human publish gate)
